ABOUT BARANGAY MANTUYONG

Barangay Mantuyong is an official barangay under Mandaue City, Cebu, part of Metro Cebu in Central Visayas, Philippines.

Location: Eastern part of Mandaue City, near Cebu City boundaries. Postal code: 6014.

Population: ~5,487 residents (2020 census).

Type: Urban barangay, residential & mixed-use.

Services: Issues clearances, certificates, permits, manages peace & order, community programs.
